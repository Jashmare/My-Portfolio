## Scope

`index.html`, the whole single-page portfolio. Visitor mode: Experience (the work leads), held to a recruiter's skim.

## Audience and job

Hiring managers and recruiters for Instructional Designer / E-learning Developer roles. Job: judge the craft in under a minute, open a project, download the CV or copy contact details.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Claim and proof. Every sentence about Prince is immediately followed by the screen that proves it. Refuses the creative-portfolio template (orange blob, arcs, tilted stat badge, marquee, identical icon cards).

OWN-WORLD: One neutral scale, near-white #F7F7F5 to near-black #111111, with a single lime accent (#B5D334 family) used only as the live dot on primary buttons, the active-state indicator and focus. Satoshi, tight tracking, headings and subheaders in Title Case (owner request): 700 for display, 500 for UI and labels, 400 kept for running body copy only (legibility at reading sizes). Grouped lists (toolkit) use the world's hairline table idiom; tool marks keep their real brand colour at small size for recognition. Screens sit unframed on the page ground (owner removed the boxes) with 14px corners and one soft shadow. The hero headline's key word carries a 60% lime marker (owner-approved exception). Buttons: solid ink primary with the lime dot, hairline ghost secondary.

STORY: The visitor reads one plain sentence on what Prince does, sees stacked real course screens as proof, scans four featured claim/proof project pairs, then a compact 2x2 More Work grid of smaller collections, sees the grouped toolkit, meets the person behind it, then copies an email or downloads the CV.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Left 5 columns: name line, headline "Ideas, Drawn to Be Understood." with "Understood" on the lime marker, the owner's one-sentence description, primary "See the work" plus ghost "Download CV". Right 7 columns: three real course screens layered as overlapping frames (course template Blue_Design_Main front with its header bar cropped, Cotton behind, Sales course back; owner chose the template for the front), bleeding to the right edge. A hairline row beneath lists the five disciplines.

FORM: Monochrome product marketing, dealt challenger (competitive), chosen by the user over the assigned Spec Sheet; seed key be7c60a8. Signature interaction: hovering a project's proof frame lifts it and fans its next two screens out from behind it; clicking opens the gallery, then fullscreen. Motion grammar: a soft rise as each proof enters, exponential ease-out, content visible by default.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

## Constraints

Keep gallery modal, fullscreen viewer, button interaction animation, light/dark toggle, CV download, copy email/phone. Hide unfinished projects. Toolkit grouped by purpose. No invented numbers.
