## Scope

`index.html`, the whole single-page portfolio. Visitor mode: Experience (the work leads), held to a recruiter's skim.

## Audience and job

Hiring managers and recruiters for Instructional Designer / E-learning Developer roles. Job: judge the craft in under a minute, open a project, download the CV or copy contact details.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Claim and proof. Every sentence about Prince is immediately followed by the screen that proves it. Refuses the creative-portfolio template (orange blob, arcs, tilted stat badge, marquee, identical icon cards).

OWN-WORLD: One neutral scale, near-white #F7F7F5 to near-black #111111, with a single lime accent (#B5D334 family) used only as the live dot on primary buttons, the active-state indicator and focus. Satoshi, sentence case, tight tracking: 700 for display, 500 for UI and labels, 400 kept for running body copy only (legibility at reading sizes). Grouped lists (toolkit) use the world's hairline table idiom; tool marks keep their real brand colour at small size for recognition. Screens sit on soft radial neutral grounds inside 14px-radius frames with one soft shadow. Buttons: solid ink primary with the lime dot, hairline ghost secondary.

STORY: The visitor reads one plain sentence on what Prince does, sees stacked real course screens as proof, scans six claim/proof project pairs, sees the grouped toolkit, meets the person behind it, then copies an email or downloads the CV.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Left 5 columns: name line, headline "I design the learning, then I draw it.", one supporting sentence, primary "See the work" plus ghost "Download CV". Right 7 columns: three real course screens layered as overlapping frames (Takaful front, Cotton behind, Data Privacy back), bleeding to the right edge. A hairline row beneath lists the five disciplines.

FORM: Monochrome product marketing, dealt challenger (competitive), chosen by the user over the assigned Spec Sheet; seed key be7c60a8. Signature interaction: hovering a project's proof frame lifts it and fans its next two screens out from behind it; clicking opens the gallery, then fullscreen. Motion grammar: a soft rise as each proof enters, exponential ease-out, content visible by default.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

## Constraints

Keep gallery modal, fullscreen viewer, button interaction animation, light/dark toggle, CV download, copy email/phone. Hide unfinished projects. Toolkit grouped by purpose. No invented numbers.
