## Design Specificity Verdict

**LLM assessment**: Mixed. The composition and interaction language (orange blob, concentric arcs, tilted stat badge, scrolling ticker, pill nav/CTAs) is a highly recognizable "creative portfolio template" pattern — visually close to many Framer/Dribbble portfolio kits. What genuinely feels authored: the About section copy ("There is a life behind all that work."), the real e-learning tool references (Storyline 360, Articulate 360), and the project modal engineering (focus trap, skeleton loader, keyboard nav) — a level of care above typical templates. The "soul" lives in the About copy and modal craftsmanship; the overall visual identity feels borrowed rather than derived from this person's specific practice.

## Overall Impression

The site has real craft underneath a template-shaped surface: the project modal (focus trap, skeleton loading, keyboard gallery nav) and the About section copy show genuine attention, but the hero's decorative chrome, the tool-grid mixing design software with AI coding tools, and two "Coming Soon" placeholders sitting inside the primary work grid all pull against the goal of a recruiter trusting this is finished, specific, professional work. The single biggest opportunity: the contact section — the actual conversion point — currently fails for keyboard/screen-reader users on 2 of 3 channels, which is a hard block, not a polish item.

## What's Working

1. **Project modal/gallery** — real focus trap, Escape-to-close, click-outside, arrow-key image nav, skeleton loader, and focus-return to the triggering card. Meaningfully more considered than the visual chrome around it.
2. **About section copy** — "There is a life behind all that work." and the Curiosity/Family/Still Becoming triad read as genuinely personal, not template filler.
3. **Dark mode** — a full parallel CSS custom-property palette (not an inverted filter), persisted via localStorage with smooth cross-fade transitions.

## Priority Issues

**[P0] Email/Phone contact "buttons" are keyboard- and screen-reader-inaccessible**
Why it matters: `.contact-link-box` for Email/Phone are plain `<div onclick>` with no `tabindex`/`role`/keydown handler — only LinkedIn is a real `<a>`. Keyboard-only and most screen-reader users cannot reach or activate 2 of the 3 contact methods, blocking the site's entire conversion goal for a whole user class. Confirmed independently by the detector's `low-contrast`/accessibility-adjacent evidence pattern and by manual source inspection.
Fix: convert to real `<button>` elements or `mailto:`/`tel:` links with a separate copy-icon affordance.
Suggested command: `$impeccable harden`

**[P0] Accent orange fails text contrast on light backgrounds**
Why it matters: `#FF8C42` as text (hero "Experiences" span, About Instagram link) measures ~2.3:1 against `#fffcfa` — below both the 4.5:1 AA text minimum and the 3:1 large-text minimum. Confirmed by the detector (7 low-contrast findings, including this exact pair) and manual CSS inspection.
Fix: introduce a darker `--accent-text` (~#B85C1E) for text usage; keep the bright orange for backgrounds/icons/decoration only.
Suggested command: `$impeccable audit`

**[P1] Placeholder/unfinished projects shown as real work in the primary grid**
Why it matters: "Logos and Branding" and "Coming Soon" render identically to finished case studies inside Selected Projects — the one section the site exists to deliver. A recruiter scanning the work hits empty promises mid-evaluation.
Fix: remove placeholders until real content exists, or visually demote them (muted/grayscale, "In progress" tag, moved to the end).
Suggested command: `$impeccable clarify`

**[P2] Tools grid mixes design software with AI coding tools**
Why it matters: Photoshop/Illustrator/Storyline/Articulate/Canva sit in the same undifferentiated grid as "Claude Code"/"Anti-Gravity," muddying the professional identity presented to a design-hiring audience.
Fix: split into labeled groups ("Design Tools" vs. "AI-Assisted Workflow") or drop the AI tools for this audience.
Suggested command: `$impeccable layout`

**[P2] All critical assets are a single external point of failure with zero fallback**
Why it matters: profile photo, CV PDF, and every project image load from `raw.githubusercontent.com` on a separate repo; no `<img>` has an `onerror` handler. If that repo is renamed, made private, or rate-limited, the visual identity and CV download silently break.
Fix: self-host assets next to `index.html`; add `onerror` fallbacks wherever external URLs remain.
Suggested command: `$impeccable harden`

**[P3] Clipboard-failure path breaks the crafted experience**
Why it matters: success uses a custom slide-in toast; failure falls through to a native `alert()` — a jarring, inconsistent interruption on an otherwise polished surface.
Fix: reuse the toast component for the failure message.
Suggested command: `$impeccable polish`

**[P3] No `prefers-reduced-motion` handling anywhere**
Why it matters: the skills ticker auto-scrolls infinitely (pauses only on hover, not on mobile/keyboard focus), alongside entrance/pop-in animations, with no reduced-motion escape hatch. Confirmed by the detector's `marquee` finding plus manual inspection (no matching media query exists in the stylesheet).
Fix: add `@media (prefers-reduced-motion: reduce)` to disable/shorten the ticker and other continuous animation.
Suggested command: `$impeccable animate`

## Persona Red Flags

The three visually-identical contact cards behave three different ways (copy / copy / open-new-tab) with no visible cue which is which. Hits "Coming Soon" while scanning the work grid and may conclude the whole site is unfinished.
